True North News Alliance Rally

April 7, 2026 · Minnesota State Capitol Rotunda

Gather with journalists, community leaders, advocates, and civic partners at the Capitol to champion something every Minnesotan deserves: reliable local news, no matter where they live.

Thriving local journalism keeps communities informed, voters engaged, and neighbors empowered to influence the decisions that shape their lives. But when the local news ecosystem erodes, so does Minnesotans' connection to their local government and civic life.

9:00 – 9:30 a.m. Networking & Tabling
9:30 – 10:30 a.m. Speaking Program Voices from across Minnesota's news landscape
10:30 – 11:30 a.m. Networking & Tabling

The Alliance is advancing three policy priorities: workforce development to build the next generation of journalists, media literacy to help communities navigate information, and sustainable funding for civic information.
