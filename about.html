Our Mission

The True North News Alliance is dedicated to strengthening local journalism across Minnesota. We bring together news organizations, nonprofits, educational institutions, and community partners to advocate for policies and practices that support sustainable local news.

Our Vision

We envision a Minnesota where every community has access to quality local journalism that informs, engages, and empowers residents to participate in civic life.

What We Do

The Alliance works to:

  • Advocate for policies that support local journalism at the state and local level
  • Connect news organizations with resources, funding opportunities, and best practices
  • Engage communities across Minnesota to understand their local news needs
  • Build partnerships between news organizations, nonprofits, and educational institutions
  • Raise awareness about the importance of local journalism to democracy and community wellbeing
