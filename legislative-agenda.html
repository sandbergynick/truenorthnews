Our Policy Priorities

The True North News Alliance advocates for policies at the state and local level that support sustainable local journalism. Our legislative agenda focuses on creating conditions for local news to thrive and serve Minnesota communities.

Priority Areas

1. Public Notice Modernization

Supporting policies that ensure public notices reach the widest possible audience while sustaining local news outlets that publish them.

2. State Advertising Support

Advocating for state advertising dollars to be directed toward local news outlets, helping sustain journalism while effectively reaching Minnesota residents.

3. Tax Incentives for Local News

Exploring tax policies that encourage investment in local journalism, including incentives for subscribers, advertisers, and news organizations.

4. Civic Information Funding

Supporting public investment in civic information infrastructure that helps communities access essential local news and information.
