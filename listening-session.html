Executive Summary

The True North News Alliance conducted listening sessions across Minnesota to understand the local news needs, challenges, and opportunities in communities throughout the state. This report summarizes our findings and recommendations.

Background

Local journalism is essential to informed communities and healthy democracy. As news organizations face economic challenges, many Minnesota communities have experienced a decline in local news coverage. The True North News Alliance launched a series of listening sessions to hear directly from community members about their experiences with local news.

Methodology

Listening sessions were conducted in communities across Minnesota, including urban, suburban, and rural areas. Participants included community members, local journalists, civic leaders, and representatives from nonprofit organizations.

Key Findings

  • Community members value local journalism and recognize its importance to civic life
  • Many residents report difficulty accessing reliable local news and information
  • Trust in local news remains higher than trust in national media
  • Communities seek news coverage that reflects their diversity and addresses their specific concerns
  • Sustainable funding models remain a significant challenge for local news organizations

Recommendations

Based on our listening sessions, the True North News Alliance recommends a multi-pronged approach to strengthening local journalism in Minnesota, including policy advocacy, community engagement, and support for innovative news models.

Next Steps

The findings from these listening sessions inform our legislative agenda and ongoing work to support local journalism across Minnesota.
